Corner Café — Q3 Review

Revenue grew 18% over last quarter, driven mainly by the new oat latte and the weekend brunch set.

Revenue

Total revenue for July–September was about $104,000, and the average ticket rose from $5.40 to $5.95. Foot traffic was flat; growth came from higher add-on rates.
