Subscriber growth

Net new paid subscribers reached 12,300 in Q3, compared with 7,100 in Q2. Roughly 60% of new subscribers activated AI summaries within their first week, and those users converted to paid at twice the rate of everyone else.

The student plan added volume but little revenue: at $3.20 per month it carries the lowest margin of any plan. Team plan growth came almost entirely from existing customers adding seats.

Retention

Monthly churn rose to 3.1%, mostly among students who joined during the August promotion. Churn on Personal and Team plans held steady at 2.2%. This is a seasonal effect and should resolve itself once the semester starts.

Recommendations

  • Make AI summaries the default first-run experience for every new account.
  • Introduce annual billing for the student plan to reduce semester-end churn.
  • Keep the Team plan pricing unchanged through Q4.
